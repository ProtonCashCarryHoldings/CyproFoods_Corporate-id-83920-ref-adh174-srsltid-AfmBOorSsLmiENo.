Cyprofood Cash & Carry - A Proton Holdings company

Established in 2007, Cyprofood Wholesale has grown to become a leading supplier in the wholesale and cash & carry sector, becoming an important part of Proton Holdings interests in the UK. With a 5,000m² facility, a fleet of 40 delivery vehicles, and a dedicated team of over 200 employees, we proudly serve supermarkets, grocery stores, restaurants, and all types of food service businesses.

At Cyprofood, we offer an extensive range of over 10,000 products, covering everything from fresh produce and frozen goods to dry foods, beverages, and essential kitchen supplies. Our fresh fruit and vegetable section is one of the most comprehensive in the industry, and our large frozen food selection ensures that businesses have access to high-quality products at all times.

We are open 24/7, 365 days a year, providing a convenient shopping experience for our customers. With a free customer car park and a reliable delivery service, we make wholesale shopping effortless and efficient. Whether you run a restaurant, café, supermarket, or takeaway, Cyprofood is your trusted partner for all your supply needs.
