About us

At CyproFoods we have a long standing culture of respect and continuous improvement, which shines through in the foods we supply and our customer service. Ever since we were established in 2007 we knew that employees are central to the operation of all businesses, which is why we provide excellent employee benefits such as a 35% discount on short-dated products and a 45 minute lunch break. In 2016 we entered our second iteration, where we improved many aspects of the store, including checkout, stock management software and layout. Relationships in the community are key to our operations, we have close ties with many catering companies and restaurants in the local area.
